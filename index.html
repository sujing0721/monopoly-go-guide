Getting Started · Board Stages

Getting Started

A lot of new players use the same playbook from board 1 to board 20. In reality, priorities shift as new mechanics unlock. Knowing which stage you're in matters more than mastering any single trick.

Boards 1–4

Learn the rhythm

Complete property sets to build a baseline income, get comfortable with Shutdowns and Bank Heists, and start checking the event calendar daily until it's a habit.

Mid boards

Start being deliberate

Stop spending dice on autopilot — save them for high-multiplier events. Start following sticker trading groups; trading beats grinding for rare stickers most of the time.

Late game / Landmarks

Concentrate resources

Once Landmarks, Peg-E, and Partner Events unlock, the strategy shifts from "steady growth" to "stack resources for key events." Shields and defense matter a lot more here.

Dice Strategy

Dice Strategy

Dice are the scarcest resource in the game — how you save and spend them decides your pace more than anything else.

Where free dice come from

Daily login rewards, dice sent by friends, and community-shared free dice links are all steady sources — make claiming them a daily habit.

Don't spend on autopilot

Jumping into every event as it appears burns dice on low-value moments. Check the multiplier and the deadline before deciding to spend now.

Save for high multipliers

Double/multi-reward events have a much better payoff. For big upcoming events, start stockpiling a few days ahead so you can go all-in during the window.

Trade dice with friends

A bigger friends list means more dice gifts coming in. Active player groups or clubs make gifting back and forth the cheapest source of extra dice.

💡 Tip: free dice links change daily. Pick one or two trustworthy community sources and stick with them instead of hunting around every time.

Building & Property Strategy

Build Order

Upgrade order directly affects your cash flow and how much you lose to a Shutdown — don't max out the first building you see.

  1. 01
    Finish property sets firstCompleting a set unlocks bonus rewards, which usually beats maxing a single building.
  2. 02
    Upgrade low-level buildings firstLow-level upgrades cost less cash and return a better ratio than pushing one building to the top.
  3. 03
    Watch your Shutdown exposureHigher-level buildings are juicier targets for Shutdowns — spread your upgrades instead of stacking them all in one place.
  4. 04
    Shift into Landmarks late-gameOnce unlocked, Landmarks often out-earn further property stacking, so it's fine to lean resources that way.

Attack & Defense

Attack & Defense

Shutdowns and Bank Heists are the most direct way players interact with each other — play both sides on purpose.

⚔️ Attacking

  • Target high-level buildings with a full cash stash — the payoff is bigger.
  • Active friends usually have higher-level buildings, so they're worth checking first.
  • Check for a shield before a Bank Heist attempt so you don't waste dice on a dud.

🛡️ Defending

  • Save shields for moments you're sitting on a big, unspent cash pile.
  • Don't let cash sit around — spend it on upgrades or events so there's less to lose.
  • Spread upgrades across buildings instead of stacking everything into one high-level target.

Sticker Strategy

Sticker Album

Stickers are the long-term collection loop — completing an album pays off well, and trading almost always beats grinding for it.

Join a trading community

Active sticker trading groups are the fastest way to land rare stickers — far more efficient than pulling packs alone.

Prioritize scarce stickers

Common stickers duplicate easily. When trading, lock in the rare ones you're missing instead of spreading trades thin.

Trade hard near season end

Trading picks up right before a sticker season closes, as players rush to clear duplicates — a good window to finish an album.
