Contact Us

Get in touch

Whether you've spotted an error in the guide, want to suggest a topic we haven't covered, or have a question about how we put the site together — we'd like to hear from you.

Email: suj58810@gmail.com

What we respond to

  • Corrections — if something in the guide doesn't match what you're seeing in-game, let us know. We'll verify and update where needed.
  • Suggestions — if there's a strategy topic or event type you'd like us to cover in more depth, send it over. Popular requests get priority.
  • Partnership enquiries — if you run a MONOPOLY GO! community (Discord server, Facebook group, YouTube channel) and want to explore a collaboration, reach out with a brief description of your community.

What we don't handle

We are not able to help with:

  • In-game purchases, refunds, or billing issues — contact Scopely support for those
  • Account recovery, bans, or login problems — only the game publisher can help with these
  • Cheats, hacks, or "free dice generator" requests — we don't provide or endorse these

Response time

This is a small, volunteer-run project. We aim to reply to all messages within 5–7 business days. If you don't hear back after that, try sending a follow-up.
