About MONOPOLY GO! Strategy Hub

Who we are

MONOPOLY GO! Strategy Hub is a fan-run guide for MONOPOLY GO! players. We are not a gaming company, a news outlet, or an official partner of Scopely or Hasbro. Everything here comes from players who have spent real time in the game and wanted to write down what actually works — so other players don't have to figure it out from scratch.

The guide was created because most free strategy content online is either recycled from the same three YouTube videos or buried behind app download prompts. We wanted a single, text-based resource that covers the parts of the game that matter: dice economy, event pacing, building strategy, PvP tactics, and sticker trading.

What's on this site

  • Board-stage guidance — what to prioritise at different points in your progression
  • Dice strategy — where to save, when to spend, and how to read multipliers
  • Dice Planner tool — an interactive calculator for splitting your dice allocation
  • Event guide — descriptions of the most common event types and how to approach each
  • Build order & property strategy — how to manage cash flow and minimise Shutdown damage
  • Attack & defense — PvP tactics for Shutdowns and Bank Heists
  • Sticker strategy — how to complete albums through trading rather than grinding

How we keep content current

MONOPOLY GO! updates its event rotation regularly, so event timing and reward structures change week to week. We review the guide and update the event sections when major mechanics shift. Specific in-game timers and reward values always come from the live app — we describe the types of events and general strategy, not exact dates or point values that could become stale.

If you spot something that looks wrong or outdated, you're welcome to reach out via our contact page. Feedback from active players helps us keep the guide accurate.

What this site is not

We don't sell in-game currency, offer account boosting, or provide any "hack" or "cheat" tools. Any such service is against MONOPOLY GO! Terms of Service and puts accounts at risk. All content here is strategy guidance only — you play the game normally, we just help you play it more deliberately.
