Free Dice: What's Real and What's a Scam

"MONOPOLY GO! free dice" is one of the most-searched phrases in the game — and most of the results are traps. Here's the honest version: dice never come from a "generator". They come from a handful of reliable systems you can run every day.

Written by the MONOPOLY GO! Strategy Hub editorial team — active players who test every routine in their own accounts before publishing it.
Reviewed and updated: 11 October 2026 · How we research and update our guides

The Honest Version

Read First

There is no tool that adds dice to your account — anyone claiming otherwise is selling ads, harvesting data, or both. What does exist are free, in-game systems that pay dice daily, and players who run them consistently collect thousands per month without spending anything.

⚠️ Golden rule: a legitimate dice reward never asks for your login, never asks you to download anything, and never asks you to complete "verification" surveys. If a site does any of those, close the tab.

The Daily Sources — Ranked by Payoff

Sources

Ranked for what most players realistically get out of them. The top source costs zero and almost everyone underuses it.

Source 1

Friend gifts (biggest by far)

Friends exchange dice gifts daily, and a full list of 200 slots generates a steady flow just from the mechanic alone. Fill it: swap codes in the game's community groups, prune players who go silent, and replace them. Accept every gift the moment you open the app — unclaimed ones pile up but sent ones build reciprocity. This alone can add several hundred dice per week for free.

Source 2

Event milestone rewards

Most events pay dice back at intermediate tiers. A dice bank spent on the right event (see the events guide) partially refunds itself, so your "free" dice compound when you pick well.

Source 3

Daily rewards & chests

Small, constant, and easy to skip — which is exactly why they matter. Make claiming them step one of every session, before any spending decisions.

Source 4

Official social channels & community links

Scopely and established content creators occasionally post legitimate dice links and codes through official social accounts. These are real but sporadic — treat them as a bonus, not a plan. Pick one or two trustworthy community sources and stick with them instead of hunting around daily.

Source 5

Album & set completion rewards

Completing sticker albums and property sets returns dice as part of the reward. Trading stickers (rather than grinding packs) accelerates this loop — the sticker guide covers how.

Spotting a Dice Generator Scam

Scam Watch

Fake dice sites share a pattern. Run any "free dice" page through this checklist before you click anything:

❌ It asks for your login

No real reward needs your account credentials. Anything that asks you to "connect your account" to receive dice is harvesting logins — walk away.

❌ It wants a download or install

Legit dice come as in-game codes or gifts. A site that says you need an app, extension, or "verification tool" is not giving you dice.

❌ It promises unlimited dice

Any "generator" claiming unlimited dice for every player, every day, is describing a broken economy. The game doesn't work that way.

✅ The real one just opens the game

Legitimate community links either open a reward inside the app itself or share a code you type in-game. One click, no forms, no waiting "counter".

💡 If you've already entered credentials on a dice site: change your account password immediately and check whatever service you used to sign in (Facebook, Apple, Google Play) for unfamiliar connected apps or sessions.

Making It a Habit

Routine

Free dice are a trickle, not a flood — the players who benefit most are the ones who never miss the trickle. Pair this page with the 5-minute daily routine in the dice guide: claim, reciprocate, check the calendar, spend with a target.

Set one daily time

Coffee, commute, before bed — one fixed slot covers claims and gift swaps. Random open-and-spend sessions are where free dice get missed.

Refresh your friend list monthly

Swap codes in community groups, prune accounts that haven't sent in weeks, refill the slots. A maintained list outperforms any link site.
