Editorial Standards

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This page explains who writes the guides on this site, how a piece of advice gets published, and what we do when it turns out to be wrong. We publish it because "a player wrote this down" should mean something more specific than that.

Who writes our guides

Every guide is written by members of the Strategy Hub editorial team — a small group of players who have each run accounts past board 20 and keep playing through seasons. We are not a content farm, we do not outsource writing, and no guide on this site is produced by copying another site's text.

We write under the team name rather than individual bylines because most articles are a merge of what several of us have observed. Where a single member developed a specific routine, we say so in the article.

How advice gets published

A tip goes through three stages before it appears on this site:

  • Tested in a live account. Someone on the team runs the routine for at least one full event cycle, on their own account, at their own board stage.
  • Checked against a second account. A second member repeats it on a different board stage, because advice that only works for late-game players is useless to someone on board 3.
  • Written from notes, not from memory. We keep a shared log of dice banks, event payouts and album progress so the numbers we quote are from our own records.

What we will not publish

  • No generator, hack, mod or cheat content. These break MONOPOLY GO! Terms of Service and risk account bans. Our free dice guide exists specifically to point players away from them.
  • No account-selling, boosting or currency-resale content — including links to services that offer it.
  • No advice we have not run ourselves. If we are relaying something we only read elsewhere, we label it as unverified rather than presenting it as a strategy.
  • No pay-for-placement. No community, tool or creator has ever paid to appear in a guide, and ad placement does not affect what we write.

How we keep pages current

MONOPOLY GO! changes its event rotation and reward structure constantly, so exact dates, costs and payout figures go stale within weeks. We handle this deliberately:

  • Guides describe event types, mechanics and decision rules, not fixed dates or point values.
  • Every guide page carries a visible "Reviewed and updated" date, and we re-check each page when a major mechanic changes.
  • Where a figure can drift, we tell you to confirm it in the live app rather than quoting a number we know will expire.

Corrections

If a guide no longer matches what you see in the game, that is a bug in our content and we want to know. Report it via the contact page with your board stage and what you observed; verified corrections are applied within a week and the page's update date is bumped. Corrections from readers have changed the advice on our sticker trading and dice pacing pages.

Advertising and disclosure

This site is a free, fan-run resource. If advertising is present it is served by third-party providers, and no advertiser has any say over the content on this site. Our privacy policy documents what data cookies and analytics collect and how to control it.
