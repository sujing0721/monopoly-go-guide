Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how MONOPOLY GO! Strategy Hub ("we", "us", "this site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

Lawful basis for processing (GDPR)

Where the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or UK GDPR applies, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) — for non-essential cookies used for advertising and for personalised ad delivery by our advertising partners.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — for aggregated analytics that tell us which guides are useful, for diagnosing technical faults, and for keeping the site secure. Our interest in measuring and improving a free guide is balanced against your privacy: we do not collect names, email addresses, or any identifier that lets us single you out.
  • Contract / service provision (Article 6(1)(b)) — for the small session cookie that makes the on-page search and event filters work while you browse.

If you are in the EEA or UK and advertising personalisation is not enabled for you, ads served on this site fall back to non-personalised or context-based delivery.

Information we collect

This site does not require registration or login. We do not ask you to submit personal information to access any part of the guide.

We may collect the following types of information automatically through standard web technologies:

  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent on each page, referring website, approximate geographic region, device type, and browser type. This data is aggregated and used to understand which sections of the guide are most useful.
  • Cookies: small text files placed on your device by this site and by third-party services we use (see below). Cookies help services function correctly and help us understand traffic patterns.

How we use information

  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve and expand the guide
  • To diagnose technical issues and maintain site performance
  • To serve relevant advertising through third-party ad networks

Cookies and tracking technologies

This site uses cookies set by itself and by third-party providers. Third-party providers include Google (for advertising and analytics) and potentially other ad-tech partners. Cookies are used for:

  • Remembering basic preferences (such as content filter selections during your session)
  • Aggregated analytics to measure page performance
  • Ad targeting and frequency capping by our advertising partners

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from reading the guide, but may disable the search and filter features.

Google AdSense

This site displays advertising served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.

  • Google's DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Google's advertising products on this site operate under Google's EU User Consent Policy: where a visitor is in the European Economic Area or the UK, personalised advertising requires explicit consent via a consent message, and consent state is passed to Google through Consent Mode. Where consent is withheld or unavailable, only non-personalised ads are requested.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on this site and which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising and/or to personalize the advertising content.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects standard log data and visitor behaviour information. The data is processed to produce reports about website activity. No personally identifying information is collected by Google Analytics on this site.

To learn more about how Google uses data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms page.

Third-party links

This site contains links to external websites, including in-game community resources. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of each site you visit.

Cookie inventory and retention

The cookies set on this site are limited to the following:

  • _ga, _ga_* — Google Analytics. Used to distinguish unique visitors. Retained up to 14 months.
  • _gcl_au — Google Ads conversion linker, set only when an ad is served. Retained up to 3 months.
  • Advertising cookies set by Google and its partners (for example IDE, DSID) — used for ad targeting and frequency capping. Retention is controlled by those providers; see the opt-outs below.
  • Session state for the search and filter features, held in your browser's memory and cleared when you close the tab.

We do not operate our own database of visitors and we do not retain IP addresses in identifiable form. Data we can actually see is aggregated: page, region at country level, device type, referrer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, rectify, export or delete the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was already lawfully processed. Because we hold only cookie-based, pseudonymous analytics data, the practical routes are usually faster than a formal request:

  • Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout (a browser add-on that stops collection on this and other sites).
  • Ad personalisation: Google Ads Settings, and My Ad Center to mute specific advertisers.
  • Cross-vendor choices: aboutads.info/choices (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (EEA).
  • Browser controls: blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings, or using a "Do Not Sell / Global Privacy Control" toggle where your browser offers one. We honour GPC signals by not enabling personalised advertising for that visit.

To make a written request, or to raise a complaint about how we handle data, email suj58810@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days, which is the GDPR deadline for access and erasure requests.

International transfers

Our processors — Google Analytics and Google AdSense — store and process data on servers outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA or UK, the transfer is covered by the provider's own compliance framework (Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or the UK equivalent). We choose these providers precisely because they publish that documentation; we do not separately control their infrastructure.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we make no attempt to identify individual visitors. Age thresholds differ by region — under 13 in the United States (COPPA), and under 16, or 13–15 depending on your EU member state, under the GDPR — so we treat all visitors the same way: no registration, no forms, no personal data collection beyond cookies, and advertising served under restricted-data rather than personalised mode where a visitor's age is not established.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Do not track and browser signals

This site responds to the Global Privacy Control signal. Browser-level "Do Not Track" is no longer a reliable standard and is not interpreted by our advertising partners, so if you want a hard opt-out use the links in Your rights above or block third-party cookies in your browser.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example when we add new features or change our use of third-party services. When we make changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy or how we handle data, please reach out via our contact page.
